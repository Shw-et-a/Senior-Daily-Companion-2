Step 1: Check your Wi-Fi Box (Router)

Look at your Wi-Fi box. Do you see lights on it? Make sure the black power plug in the wall switch is turned ON.

Step 2: Check the Internet Light Color

Find the light with a small globe or 'Internet' sign. Is it Green/Blue or Red/Orange?

Great news! The internet line is working.

Swipe down from the top of your phone screen. Tap the Wi-Fi icon off, wait 5 seconds, and tap it back on.

The box has lost connection.

Don't worry, 90% of the time a 30-second restart fixes it completely. Proceed to Step 3 below!

Step 3: The 30-Second Refresh Trick

Turn off the wall switch of the Wi-Fi box. Wait 30 seconds before turning it back on. Use our timer below!

TV 'No Signal' Helper

  • Grab your TV Remote (not the set-top box remote).
  • Look for a button named 'Source', 'Input', or a square with an arrow pointing inside 🔲➡️.
  • Press that button and select 'HDMI 1' or 'HDMI 2' where your cable box is plugged in.

Senior Flight Booking Guide

Everything you need to book comfortably with zero stress.

1. Senior Citizen Concession

Air India, IndiGo, and SpiceJet offer senior discounts for travelers aged 60+. Carry a government photo ID at the airport.

2. Free Wheelchair Assistance

Request free wheelchair help right when booking online or by calling the airline. Airline staff will escort you all the way.

3. Best Seat Selection

Always choose an Aisle Seat near the front of the aircraft (rows 2 to 7). It makes walking to the lavatory and deboarding much easier.

4. Cabin Baggage & Medications

Keep all your daily medicines and doctor prescriptions in your cabin handbag — NEVER put essential medications in check-in luggage!
